# Charges have a work state and a billing state

A Charge has two states, each worked out from its history (ADR 0010). Its **work state** says whether it happened: **planned** (_previsto_), **done** (_eseguito_) or **not done** (_non eseguito_). Its **billing state** says whether the Customer has been charged: to bill, in draft, billed, not billable, credited. Stock follows the work state alone; Invoices follow the billing state, which only acts on done Charges.

Stock: a planned Item Charge is **committed** (_impegnato_), still in the warehouse but spoken for, so an Item's available quantity is its stock less what is committed, and reorder alerts use what is available; a done Item Charge leaves stock when it becomes done; a not done one never touches stock. A warning, never a refusal, shows when planned work would take available below zero.

Work state: Charges on a Visit are planned (recorded in advance, or imported from a Quote) until the Visit closes, when they become done. Closing it, the Technician reviews them, all ticked, so what wasn't used is unticked and becomes not done; Office can switch a Charge between done and not done afterwards, until it is billed, and stock follows. A Visit can be closed, in the field or by Office on a cancelled Job (ADR 0015), once it is in progress, at any time, or once its start time has passed; a Visit still planned with a start to come can't be. The Visit records when it was marked in progress and when it was closed, and by whom. Its work is dated by its work day: the day (Europe/Rome) it was marked in progress, or its planned day if it never was, and never later than today; its Items leave stock, its serviced equipment is last serviced and its installed equipment is installed on that day, also when Office marks one of its Charges done later (while an Item Office marks not done comes back on stock the day Office does it, as a correction), so nothing leaves stock on a day still to come (decided by the business on 2026-10-29, #174, replacing #154's "not before its start time"). Cancelling a Visit makes its Charges not done, and restoring it makes them planned again. A cancelled Visit (_annullato_) keeps its record, takes nothing off stock, and doesn't count as work. On the Job itself, Charges Office records start done (a call-out, a contractor's day, usually entered after the fact), and Charges imported from a Quote start planned until Office confirms them used, when they leave stock (replacing ADR 0012's "Job Charges never move stock").

Installed Equipment follows the work state too (#162): an installed Item's Charge keeps the equipment's details (a new unit's type, make, model and serial number, or which unit already at the Site it goes onto) while planned or not done, and only when it becomes done, at the Visit's close or when Office marks it done, is the new equipment created at the Job's Site or the existing unit updated, installed on the Visit's work day. Done again after not done, it reuses the equipment it created, if that still exists. What changed since recording is warned of, never refused (#168): a unit marked removed meanwhile, or no longer at the Job's Site, is updated all the same (and stays removed), and a new unit isn't created when the Job no longer has a Site. From then on the equipment is managed by hand: marking the Charge not done or correcting its Item never creates, changes or removes equipment, and Office is warned instead, naming the equipment to correct or mark removed on the Site. Removing a Charge never touches equipment either: it can only be removed in the field before it was ever done, so it has installed nothing. A cancelled Visit's Charges, never done, install nothing. Decided by the business on 2026-10-29.

Billing: only done Charges can be billed; a planned Charge keeps the Job's billing open, since its work is still to come. "Not billable" can be decided at any time, even while planned (warranty work), and takes effect when done. A billed Charge is frozen, its work state and its recorded quantity alike, until a Credit Note credits it (ADR 0014): anything wrong on billed work is undone with a Credit Note first, after which it can be corrected. Setting a billed Charge back to bill (ADR 0010, for billing it again by hand) reopens only its billing side; its work state and recorded quantity stay frozen while any issued billing of it isn't credited. Decided by the business on 2026-10-29.

## Considered Options

- **One state for everything** (until now, with "not billable" or a new "not carried out" state added): "not billable" means the work happened but isn't charged, which is wrong for work that never happened, and one state can't say at once whether stock moved and whether the Customer paid.
- **Corrections to billed Charges at any time** (ADR 0010 until now): convenient, but recorded and billed values then drift apart on issued documents.
- **Job Charges as billing only, never moving stock** (ADR 0012): simple, but planned material for a Job would be invisible to stock and never leave it.

## Consequences

- A cancelled Visit status, with restore.
- Stock shows in stock, committed and available per Item. Only planned Charges on Jobs open or in progress are committed: a Job's planned Charges commit nothing while it is closed or cancelled, and reopening a closed Job commits them again (#158).
- An installed Item recorded in the field no longer appears among the Site's equipment until its Visit closes. Equipment the app created at recording time for a Charge still planned is left as it is (only dev and seed data has any; no data migration).
- Existing data: Charges on closed Visits are done, on open Visits planned; Job Charges are done, except Item Charges imported from a Quote, which are planned.
- (Refines ADR 0010's corrections "at any time" and ADR 0012's Job Item Charges.)
